Course Overview

Build Confidence with Canva

Canva is one of the most accessible design platforms for creating professional visual content without requiring advanced graphic design software.

This course introduces Canva from the beginning and guides you through the tools, features and practical workflows needed to create useful designs confidently.

The focus is not only on watching tutorials. You will be encouraged to practise, complete assignments and create projects that demonstrate what you have learned.

Learning Outcomes

What You Will Learn

✓

Navigate the Canva dashboard and understand its main tools and features.

✓

Create professional designs using templates and custom layouts.

✓

Work confidently with text, images, shapes, colours and design elements.

✓

Design flyers, posters, presentations and social media graphics.

✓

Create and edit basic video content using Canva.

✓

Develop business materials such as CVs and business cards.

✓

Prepare designs correctly for download and digital use.

✓

Apply your skills through assignments and practical projects.

Course Curriculum

What You’ll Study

The programme is structured progressively so that beginners can build confidence before moving into more practical design work.

01
Getting Started

Introduction to Canva

Understand what Canva is, what it can be used for and how the course will work.

02
Canva Fundamentals

Understanding the Canva Dashboard

Explore templates, projects, apps, design tools and the Canva workspace.

03
Design Practice

Creating with Canva Templates

Learn how to select, customise and transform Canva templates into your own professional designs.

04
Practical Design

Designing Professional Flyers

Learn layout, text placement, images, colours and visual balance through a practical flyer project.

05
Advertising Design

Posters & Billboard Designs

Create visually strong advertising designs using Canva elements, typography and product imagery.

06
Video

Canva Video Editing

Learn basic video editing, transitions, text animation, music and sound effects.

07
Presentation

Creating Professional Presentations

Build and present visually engaging presentations using Canva.

08
Career Design

Designing a Professional CV

Learn how to structure and design a clean, professional CV.

09
Business Design

Creating a Business Card

Design a professional business card with strong branding and layout.

10
Print-on-Demand

From Canva Design to Product

Learn the basic workflow for preparing a Canva design for a print-on-demand product.
