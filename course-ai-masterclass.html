Course Overview

Build Professional AI Skills

The AI Master Class is designed for learners who already understand the basic concepts of artificial intelligence and want to use AI more effectively in professional, business and practical environments.

You will move beyond simple questions and answers and learn how to structure stronger prompts, research and compare information, work with documents and data, improve productivity, create professional content and design repeatable AI-assisted workflows.

The course also introduces AI automation and workflow thinking so that you can understand how repetitive tasks, information flows and business processes can be improved with AI and connected tools.

Throughout the programme, you will complete practical exercises and projects that can demonstrate your ability to apply AI to real workplace, freelance or business needs.

Course Curriculum

What You’ll Study

The programme moves from advanced prompting and professional productivity into research, business applications, automation, responsible AI use and a final real-world project.

01
AI Skills Foundation

From Basic AI Use to Professional Work

Review essential AI concepts and learn how professional AI use differs from casual prompting.

02
Advanced Prompting

Prompt Engineering for Better Results

Build structured prompts using context, roles, constraints, examples, output formats and iterative refinement.

03
Research & Information

AI-Assisted Research & Knowledge Work

Use AI to explore topics, compare information, organise findings and develop clearer research workflows while checking important sources.

04
Documents & Data

Working with Documents, Tables & Information

Learn practical approaches for summarising documents, extracting information, comparing content and analysing structured data with AI.

05
Professional Productivity

AI for Writing, Communication & Planning

Use AI to support emails, reports, meeting preparation, planning, documentation and other professional tasks.

06
Career Applications

AI for Employment & Professional Development

Apply AI to skills analysis, CV improvement, interview preparation, learning plans and professional development without replacing your own judgement.

07
Business Applications

AI for Business & Customer Workflows

Explore practical AI uses for customer enquiries, marketing support, business communication, idea development and operational tasks.

08
Content Systems

AI for Content Creation & Marketing

Plan and create structured content workflows for websites, social media, campaigns, educational materials and business communication.

09
Automation Foundations

Understanding AI Automation & Workflows

Learn the building blocks of automation, including triggers, actions, conditions, data movement and human review.

10
Connected Workflows

Building Practical AI-Assisted Automations

Design workflow examples for administration, customer support, lead handling, reporting, content processes and IT support.

11
Responsible Professional AI

Accuracy, Privacy, Security & Human Oversight

Learn when AI output requires verification and how privacy, confidential information, bias, security and human responsibility affect professional AI use.

12
Capstone Project

Build a Real-World AI Solution

Identify a practical problem and design an end-to-end AI-assisted solution with prompts, workflow steps, outputs, safeguards and measurable goals.
