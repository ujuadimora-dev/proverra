Course Overview

From Prompt to Motion

PromptToMotionAI is a practical creative-AI course designed to show you how an idea can move through a complete digital content-production workflow.

You will learn how to turn an idea into a clear prompt, generate useful AI images, maintain visual consistency, transform still images into motion and video, and strengthen the final content with voice, music and sound.

You will also learn how tools such as Canva and CapCut can bring the different parts of an AI project together for editing, captions, branding and final delivery.

The emphasis is on practical creation. Exercises and projects will help you develop content for education, social media, business, storytelling and your own creative portfolio.

Course Curriculum

What You’ll Study

The course follows the creative process from the first idea through prompting, image generation, motion, audio, editing and final project production.

01
Creative AI Foundations

From Idea to AI Workflow

Understand the PromptToMotionAI workflow and how ideas, prompts, images, motion, audio and editing work together.

02
Prompt Foundations

Writing Effective Creative Prompts

Learn how to structure prompts with subject, setting, action, style, composition, lighting and other useful details.

03
Visual Prompting

AI Image Generation

Turn written ideas into useful AI images and learn how to refine prompts when the first result does not match your goal.

04
Visual Consistency

Characters, Scenes & Style

Plan recurring characters, clothing, environments and visual direction so multi-scene projects feel more consistent.

05
Story Planning

Scenes, Storyboards & Shot Planning

Break a concept into scenes and plan the images, actions, camera views and assets needed before generating motion.

06
Prompt to Motion

Image-to-Video Fundamentals

Transform still images into short motion clips and understand how movement, timing and scene direction affect the result.

07
Motion Prompting

Camera & Movement Prompts

Write clearer prompts for subject movement, camera movement, expressions and cinematic actions without overloading a scene.

08
AI Voice

Voiceovers & Narration

Prepare scripts and use AI or recorded voiceovers to add narration, dialogue and explanation to visual content.

09
Audio Production

Music & Sound Effects

Use background music and sound effects purposefully to support mood, pacing, storytelling and audience attention.

10
Editing Workflow

Canva & CapCut Integration

Bring images, clips, voice, music, captions and branding together and edit them into a polished final video.

11
Content Applications

Social, Educational & Business Content

Apply the workflow to short-form videos, learning content, promotional media, storytelling and other practical use cases.

12
Portfolio Project

Complete PromptToMotionAI Production

Plan and produce a complete project from initial idea and prompts through visual generation, motion, audio, editing and final export.

Practical Learning

Assignments & Creative Projects

The projects are designed to help you practise each stage of the workflow and build finished examples of your work.

01

Prompt-to-Image Challenge

Develop a creative concept, write structured prompts and produce a small set of purposeful AI visuals.

02

Image-to-Motion Project

Take a still visual through scene planning, motion prompting and image-to-video generation.

03

Complete AI Content Project

Produce a finished piece of content combining prompts, visuals, motion, voice or audio, editing and branding.
