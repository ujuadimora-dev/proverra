WHO WE ARE

An Academy Built Around Practical Skills

Proverra Academy was created to make practical digital and professional learning more accessible to people who want to develop useful skills and create new opportunities.

Our learning areas include design, artificial intelligence, technology, digital marketing, business, website development, content creation, data, cybersecurity, cloud computing, languages, leadership and professional development.

Courses are designed to combine clear instruction with practical exercises, assignments and projects so learners can move from understanding a topic to actually using it.
