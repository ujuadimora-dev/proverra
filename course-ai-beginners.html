Course Overview

Build Confidence with Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is changing the way people learn, work, communicate, create content and solve problems. Understanding how AI works and how to use it effectively is becoming an important digital skill.

This beginner-friendly course introduces Artificial Intelligence from the fundamentals and gradually moves into practical applications. You will learn what AI is, how it differs from traditional software, the major types of AI and how AI is already being used in everyday life and across different industries.

You will also begin working with modern AI tools, learn how to communicate with AI through effective prompts, explore generative AI and discover practical ways to apply AI to learning, career development, business, productivity and creative work.

The focus is not simply on watching lessons. You will practise what you learn through exercises, assignments and practical projects designed to help you build confidence using AI responsibly and effectively.

Course Curriculum

What You’ll Study

The programme starts with the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and gradually moves into practical AI tools, prompting, generative AI and real-world applications.

01
AI Foundations

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Understand what Artificial Intelligence is, how it has developed and how AI is already influencing everyday and professional life.

02
Understanding AI

Types of Artificial Intelligence

Explore Narrow AI, General AI and Super AI, and distinguish these capability levels from approaches such as Generative AI and Predictive AI.

03
AI in the Real World

AI Around Us

Discover how AI is used in search engines, recommendations, navigation, voice assistants, healthcare, education, business and other everyday services.

04
Responsible AI

Benefits & Limitations of AI

Understand the advantages of AI, where AI can make mistakes and why verification, privacy, responsible use and human judgement are important.

05
AI Communication

Introduction to Prompt Engineering

Learn how to communicate clearly with AI, structure effective prompts and improve instructions to receive more useful results.

06
AI Tools

Working with Modern AI Tools

Explore practical AI tools for research, writing, brainstorming, communication, learning and productivity.

07
Generative AI

AI for Text, Images, Audio & Video

Discover how Generative AI can help create written content, images, audio, video concepts and other forms of digital content.

08
Professional Applications

AI for Work, Career & Business

Explore practical ways to use AI for job preparation, professional tasks, business, entrepreneurship and productivity.

09
AI & Your Skills

Building Opportunities with AI

Learn how AI can complement your existing skills and explore opportunities across creative, business, technical, freelance and professional fields.

10
Practical Application

AI Projects & Skills Practice

Apply what you have learned through practical exercises and beginner-friendly projects that demonstrate your ability to use AI effectively.

Practical Learning

Assignments & Projects

Proverra Academy encourages learners to practise throughout the course. These activities help you move from simply understanding AI to using it confidently for practical tasks.

01

AI Prompt Challenge

Write and improve prompts for different tasks such as learning, writing, research, planning and professional communication.

02

AI Productivity Project

Choose a real task and use an AI tool to help research, organise, create or improve the work. Review the AI output before producing your final result.

03

AI + Your Skills Project

Choose one of your existing skills or career interests and create a practical example showing how AI can support or improve that area.
