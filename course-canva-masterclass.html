Course Overview

Build Professional Design & Business Skills

The Canva Business Master Class is designed for learners who already understand the basics of Canva and want to use their design skills more professionally for business, freelancing, marketing and entrepreneurship.

You will move beyond basic design and learn how to create consistent brand identities, professional marketing materials, social media content systems, presentations, client work and print-on-demand products.

The programme also introduces practical business workflows, including preparing products for Printify, understanding online selling channels and building a portfolio that demonstrates real-world design and business skills.

This is a practical master class. You will complete assignments and projects that can support your portfolio, freelance services or your own business.

Course Curriculum

What You’ll Study

The master class progresses from professional Canva workflows into branding, marketing, client work, print-on-demand, online selling and portfolio development.

01
Business Foundations

Canva for Business & Professional Work

Understand how Canva can support branding, marketing, freelancing, client services and business communication.

02
Brand Strategy

Professional Branding & Brand Kits

Build a consistent visual identity using colours, typography, logos and reusable brand elements.

03
Marketing Design

Business Marketing Materials

Create professional flyers, posters, promotional graphics and other materials for real business campaigns.

04
Content Marketing

Social Media Content Systems

Design coordinated social media content and develop reusable templates for consistent brand communication.

05
Professional Communication

Business Presentations & Proposals

Create polished presentations, proposals and visual documents for clients, teams and business opportunities.

06
Client Work

Client Design Workflows

Learn how to organise a design brief, plan deliverables, manage revisions and prepare professional files for clients.

07
Print-on-Demand

Introduction to Print-on-Demand

Understand the print-on-demand business model and how Canva designs can be prepared for physical products.

08
Product Creation

Creating Products with Canva

Create product-ready designs for items such as T-shirts and other print-on-demand products.

09
Printify Workflow

Connecting Canva Designs with Printify

Learn how to upload designs, create products, prepare mockups and understand production costs and pricing.

10
Selling Online

Etsy & Online Store Workflow

Explore selling platforms, Etsy costs and fees, store setup, connecting Printify and publishing your first product.

11
Professional Growth

Building Your Design Portfolio

Select and present your strongest work in a professional portfolio for freelancing, employment or business.

12
Capstone Project

Final Business Project

Combine branding, marketing and product-design skills in a complete practical business project.
